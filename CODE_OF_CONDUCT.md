# Code of conduct

BuddhistAI brings together people from many Buddhist traditions, languages and professions: monastics and lay practitioners, translators, scholars, developers and funders. We want everyone to feel welcome and to be treated with care.

## Our commitments

- **Be kind and patient.** Many contributors write in their second or third language, and many are new to GitHub. Assume good intentions, explain rather than correct, and help people finish what they started.
- **Respect every tradition.** No tradition is the default. Present a tradition's view as that tradition's view, attribute positions, and do not argue which school is right. Disagreements about doctrine belong in the sources, not in personal debate.
- **Cite your sources.** Glossaries, answer keys and skills should say where their terms and readings come from, so others can check them.
- **Honour restrictions on texts.** Do not share material whose transmission is restricted (for example texts that require empowerment or permission), or copyrighted editions and translations without permission. When unsure, ask a maintainer first.
- **Credit people.** Name the teachers, translators, editors and projects whose work you build on, and the people who reviewed yours.
- **Be honest about results.** Report scores as measured, including bad ones. Never tune on test data or misstate what a skill can do.
- **No harassment.** No insults, personal attacks, sexualised language, discrimination, or publishing others' private information.

## Scope

This applies in every BuddhistAI repository, issue, pull request and discussion, and when representing BuddhistAI elsewhere.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately via [a private security advisory](https://github.com/BuddhistAI/buddhist-skills/security/advisories/new) or by messaging a maintainer listed in [CODEOWNERS](./.github/CODEOWNERS). Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove content and, if needed, block people who do not follow this code.
